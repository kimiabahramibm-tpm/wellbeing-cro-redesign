# Wellbeing CRO Redesign

A Product Manager take-home assignment focused on improving purchase confidence and reducing conversion friction on an ecommerce product page.

## Objective

Identify high-impact sources of purchase friction, prioritize optimization opportunities, and translate the recommendations into a testable product concept.

## Deliverables

- CRO Audit
- Prioritized Optimization Opportunities
- Product Page Redesign
- HTML/CSS Prototype
- Experimentation Plan
